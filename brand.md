**Version 1.0**  
_Rooted in reliability. Built for growth._

## 1. Brand Essence

**RootCommerce** represents a strong foundation and organic growth.  
Just like roots anchor a tree and enable it to grow taller, this theme gives online stores a solid, reliable base to scale confidently.

### Brand Personality

- Grounded & Trustworthy
- Modern Organic
- Clean & Professional
- Growth-oriented
- Reliable

### Brand Voice

Clear, confident, calm, and supportive.  
Avoid hype. Speak like a reliable partner who helps businesses grow steadily.

## 3. Color Palette

| Role           | Color Name        | Hex       | Usage                             |
| -------------- | ----------------- | --------- | --------------------------------- |
| Primary        | Deep Forest Green | `#1B4332` | Logo, primary buttons, headers    |
| Secondary      | Warm Clay         | `#8B5E3C` | Accents, icons, secondary buttons |
| Accent         | Soft Sage         | `#A3B18A` | Highlights, success states        |
| Highlight      | Warm Amber        | `#D4A373` | Hover states, badges, CTAs        |
| Background     | Soft Cream        | `#F8F5F0` | Page & section backgrounds        |
| Text Primary   | Charcoal          | `#2D2D2D` | Body text, headings               |
| Text Secondary | Warm Gray         | `#6B6B6B` | Secondary text, captions          |
| White          | Pure White        | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, pure backgrounds           |

### Color Usage Rules

- Primary green should dominate branding and main CTAs.
- Warm Clay and Amber are used sparingly for energy and warmth.
- Soft Cream is preferred over pure white for a more organic feel.
- Maintain strong contrast for accessibility (WCAG AA minimum).

## 4. Typography

### Primary Typeface

**Inter** (or similar geometric sans-serif: Satoshi, Geist, or Plus Jakarta Sans)

| Style   | Weight         | Use Case                 |
| ------- | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| Display | Bold (700)     | Logo, large headings     |
| Heading | SemiBold (600) | Section titles           |
| Body    | Regular (400)  | Paragraphs, product text |
| Caption | Medium (500)   | Labels, small text       |

### Optional Elegant Pairing

- **Root** → Refined serif (e.g. Fraunces or Playfair Display)
- **Commerce** → Clean sans-serif (Inter)

### Typography Rules

- Keep line height generous (1.5–1.6 for body text).
- Use sentence case for most headings.
- Avoid all-caps except for very short labels.

## 5. Logo System

### Primary Wordmark

Clean modern wordmark with subtle root motifs emerging from the base of the letters (especially the “R” and “C”).  
Color: Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`) on light backgrounds.  
White or cream version available for dark backgrounds.

### Logo Mark / Icon

Abstract stylized roots forming a circular modern shape that suggests both stability and growth.  
Works perfectly as:

- Favicon
- App icon
- Social media avatar
- Loading indicator

### Logo Clear Space

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the letter “R” around the logo on all sides.

### Logo Do’s & Don’ts

**Do:**

- Use the full wordmark on website headers and marketing materials
- Use the icon alone on small sizes (favicon, app icons)
- Maintain original proportions

**Don’t:**

- Stretch or distort the logo
- Change the colors outside the approved palette
- Add drop shadows or effects
- Place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
